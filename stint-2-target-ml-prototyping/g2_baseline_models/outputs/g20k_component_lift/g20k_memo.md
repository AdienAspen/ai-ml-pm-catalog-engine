# G2.0k diagnostic lift

G2.0k.1 is not applicable to the frozen G2.0i pipeline: order text was matched by exact, untruncated phrases. MiniLM embedded short concept labels and IH06 descriptions, not full order text. The 702 long eligible B1 orders include 234 already resolved and 468 unresolved. A windowed full-text semantic detector would be a new instrument, not a controlled segmentation rerun.

G2.0k.2 adds 12 G1.1e-derived phrase concepts and yields 1 additional diagnostic profile candidates among 459 affected unresolved orders, at the unchanged 0.60 score and 0.05 margin. This is not precision, independent FLOC evidence, or catalogue-code accuracy. The 20 G1.1e reviewed orders are excluded from future Object Part evaluation. The source is masked before normalization. No F6 change or candidate promotion.
